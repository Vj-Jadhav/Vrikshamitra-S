How AQI is Calculated

In the game, AQI changes based on the buildings you place:

• 🏠 Houses: +2 AQI
• 🏭 Factories: +15 AQI
• ⚫ Coal Plants: +25 AQI
• 🌳 Parks: -5 AQI
• 💨 Wind Turbines: -12 AQI
• ☀️ Solar Farms: -10 AQI
• 🚲 Bike Lanes: -7 AQI
